27/**
28 * Solve the linear elasticity equation written for the mesh position :
29 *
30 * - \nabla \cdot \sigma(x) + f = 0,
31 *
32 * with \sigma(x) = 2\mu\epsilon(x) + \lambda\tr{\epsilon(x)} I.
33 * The infinitesimal strain tensor \epsilon(x) is written in terms of the mesh
34 * position, and not in terms of displacement u, and thus writes:
35 *
36 * \epsilon(x) = (\nabla(u) + \nabla(u)^T)/2
37 * = (\nabla(x) + \nabla(x)^T)/2 - I
38 *
39 * The source term f can either be evaluated on the reference configuration X,
40 * or on the current configuration x(X), yielding a nonlinear problem.
41 * For the latter, the nonlinear solver may fail to find a solution if the
42 * source term on the current mesh is too steep. To counteract this, a
43 * continuation method is used, to progressively account for the source term,
44 * and we actually solve :
45 *
46 * - \nabla \cdot \sigma(x) + alpha * f(x(X)) = 0.
47 *
48 * This feature is controlled by the "Elasticity" subsection of the
49 * parameter file. The continuation parameter alpha lies in the provided
50 * [min_coeff, max_coeff] bracket, so that the last solved position field
51 * satisfies :
52 *
53 * - \nabla \cdot \sigma(x) + max_coeff * f(x(X)) = 0.
54 *
55 */

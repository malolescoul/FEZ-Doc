8/**
9 * A ComponentOrdering describes the lower and upper component indices for a
10 * given solver. For now, all solvers work in a monolithic fashion, solving
11 * their complete system of PDEs in a unique coupled system.
12 *
13 * Each derived class must specify the [lower, upper) interval for its
14 * variables.
15 *
16 * FIXME: These orderings should be known at compile time.
17 */

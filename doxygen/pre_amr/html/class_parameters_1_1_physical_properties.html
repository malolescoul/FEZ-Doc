◆ body_force

template<int dim>
Tensor<1, dim> Parameters::PhysicalProperties< dim >::body_force

Body force vector (e.g., gravitational acceleration). In solvers where the momentum equation is divided by density (incompressible single-fluid NS), this is used directly as a kinematic acceleration. In solvers with variable density (CHNS, compressible NS), it is multiplied by the local density to obtain the volumetric force term.

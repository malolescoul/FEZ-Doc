16/**
17 * The purpose of this class is to compute the next time step of an unsteady
18 * simulation based on the trunaction error of the BDF time stepping methods.
19 * For a method of order p, the truncation error of order p + 1 writes :
20 *
21 * e \simeq (-1)^{p+1} h (\sum_{i=0}^p \alpha_i H_i^{p+1}) [u^{n+1}, \ldots,
22 * u^{n+1-(p+1)}],
23 *
24 * where h is the current time step, the \alpha_i are the BDF coefficients,
25 * H_i := t_{n+1} - t_{n+1-i} and the brackets [., ..., .] are the (forward)
26 * divided differences of the solution u at times n+1 to n+1-(p+1).
27 *
28 * This error estimate is combined with the a priori relation e_n \propto
29 * h_n^{p+1} to obtain an estimation of the next time step :
30 *
31 * \frac{e_{n+1}}{e_n} \approx \left(\frac{h_{n+1}}{h_n}\right)^{p+1}.
32 *
33 * Letting \epsilon denote the target error e_{n+1}, this yield the time step
34 *
35 * h_{n+1} \approx h_n * \left(\frac{\epsilon}{e_n}\right)^{\frac{1}{p+1}}.
36 *
37 * The estimation of e_n requires an additional solution vector, which is
38 * stored in this class.
39 */
